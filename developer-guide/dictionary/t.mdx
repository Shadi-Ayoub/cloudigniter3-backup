# T


## Tenant

An isolated application/customer scope with a stable identity, slug, lifecycle, routing context, and scoped authorization assignments. A Tenant may attach to several Org Units, including nodes shared with other tenants.


## Tenant Attachment

The relationship that makes an Org Unit available within a Tenant. The child node’s tenant set must remain a subset of its direct parent’s tenant set.


## Tenant-aware Route

A logical feature route rendered through a Tenant or Org Unit public pathname. The proxy validates the scope and rewrites it to the internal Tenant route tree without exposing that physical prefix to application links.


## Tenant Context

The trusted request-specific Tenant information resolved before route matching. It participates in routing, localization, data access, and authorization but does not contain the complete Tenant inventory.


## Tenant Scope

An authorization boundary limited to one Tenant. Tenant identity must match between the assignment and the requested runtime scope.


## Trash

The authorized management area for soft-deleted resources. It offers Restore and, when separately permitted, irreversible purge with exact confirmation and a reason.


## Trusted Boundary

A server, handler, resolver, job, or provider operation that re-resolves identity, scope, authorization, and mutation invariants instead of trusting browser presentation state.
