# E


## EmberGuard

CloudIgniter’s provider-neutral authentication and authorization capability. Its internal algorithms live in `packages/emberguard`, while supported generic APIs are exposed through `@cloudigniter/core`. See [Access Control](/docs/core-system/authorization/overview).


## Environment Mode

The resolved operating mode—such as development, test, staging, or production—that controls environment-sensitive behavior. Development tooling requires the exact `development` mode and cannot be enabled by a browser-only check.


## Exact Propagation

An assignment propagation rule that applies only at its declared scope. For an Org Unit assignment, it does not grant access to child Org Units.
